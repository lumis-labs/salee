# Salee autonomous growth capabilities

Salee Arman is configured to pursue revenue for the default productized service, the **AI Revenue Workflow Sprint**.

The growth loop runs at low frequency (`GROWTH_INTERVAL_HOURS`, default six hours) and uses one small specialist call. It combines current operational metrics with the existing published artifacts and produces:

- landing-page headline/subheadline updates;
- FAQ copy and structured Service metadata for SEO/GEO;
- useful blog/insight posts served at `/blog` and included in `/sitemap.xml`;
- positioning and packaging experiments recorded for measurement;
- sales/support replies and consented follow-ups;
- paid-customer intake and automated workflow-audit fulfillment.
- owned-channel visitor interest capture with explicit contact permission and a useful next-step reply;
- an AI-readable `/llms.txt` summary and crawl-aware sitemap/robots surfaces.

The deterministic supervisor owns publication, deduplication, provider limits, and quality control. The specialist cannot change prices or payment behavior silently. A proposed offer pivot is recorded as an experiment; a live checkout must be recreated deliberately so existing customers are not mispriced. No cold-contact scraping or unsolicited outreach is enabled.

Future adapters can add analytics, search-console data, ad attribution, CRM imports, and approved publishing targets without changing the specialist contract. Each adapter should expose only narrow read/write tools and preserve the audit log. Until a real domain, traffic source, or approved distribution adapter is connected, owned content alone cannot create meaningful inbound demand.
